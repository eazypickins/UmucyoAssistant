## How to use these lists

1. These are BUYERS (procuring entities) - approach them as the organizations who publish tenders and need tender-management/compliance support.
2. All contacts came from RPPA OCDS procurement records in BigQuery - they are the actual tender contact points, not guessed.
3. For each test-drive, email 2-3 orgs in ONE industry first, offer a 15-min demo of tender discovery + compliance, and measure response.
4. Cross-reference the construction 50 (PROSPECT_UNIVERSE_50.md) for supplier-side prospects.
