\# Purpose



This document defines how EazyPickins introduces its products into the market.



Our objective is not simply to acquire customers.



Our objective is to become a trusted decision partner for African organizations.



\---



\# Market Philosophy



Organizations rarely buy software.



They buy confidence.



They buy reduced risk.



They buy saved time.



They buy better decisions.



Everything we communicate should focus on these outcomes.

\# Messaging Framework



Never begin with features.



Begin with outcomes.



Instead of:



"Our platform uses advanced AI."



Say:



"Your team spends less time searching for information and more time making informed decisions."



Instead of:



"We automate procurement."



Say:



"We reduce the effort required to find, understand, and act on procurement opportunities."
