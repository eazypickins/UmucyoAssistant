1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide and improve the Service:

  • Account data: email address, business name, sector, and company profile details you provide during onboarding.
  • Usage data: features accessed, tenders viewed and saved, AI queries submitted, and interaction patterns.
  • Payment data: subscription tier, payment method, and transaction records. Full payment card details are processed by our payment providers and are never stored on our servers.

2. How We Use Information

  • To personalize tender matches and recommendations based on your business profile.
  • To provide AI-generated insights, compliance checks, and strategy reports.
  • To process subscription payments and manage account access.
  • To improve the Service through usage analytics and feature development.
  • To send service-related communications (match alerts, subscription updates).

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in Google Sheets, Google Apps Script's built-in storage (PropertiesService), and Google BigQuery. All storage uses Google's infrastructure with standard encryption at rest and in transit. We retain your data for as long as your account is active.

4. Third-Party Services

The Service relies on the following third-party providers. Each processes data according to its own privacy policy:

  • Google Sheets & Apps Script — primary data storage and application hosting.
  • Google BigQuery — historical procurement data analytics and prediction engine.
  • OpenRouter AI — powers tender matching, compliance checking, and AI tutoring features. Queries are transmitted to OpenRouter's API for processing.
  • Stripe — processes card payments for subscriptions.
  • MTN MoMo & Airtel Money — process mobile money payments in Rwanda.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data while your subscription is active. Upon cancellation, your profile data is retained for 90 days to allow reactivation, after which it is permanently deleted. Aggregated, anonymized analytics data may be retained indefinitely.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and export your personal data.
  • Correct inaccurate profile information.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Opt out of non-essential communications.

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.
