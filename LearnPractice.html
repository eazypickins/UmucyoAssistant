⚠️ Report Procurement Corruption

If you suspect fraud, bid rigging, or corruption in a procurement process, you are protected by Rwanda's whistleblower laws. Report safely and confidentially through Transparency International Rwanda's Advocacy and Legal Advice Centre (ALAC).

📞 Call: +250 788 309 563 / +250 788 309 583
✉️ Email: info@tirwanda.org
🔗 Report online: transparency.org/report-corruption/rwanda
🏢 Visit: KN 3RD Street, Plot Nr 120, Kigali
All reports are confidential. Whistleblowers are legally protected under Rwandan law.
