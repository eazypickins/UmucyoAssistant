Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started?
Visit the app, enter your email, then complete the 5-step Business DNA wizard. This tells the app what you do, your budget range, and your preferences so it can find the best tenders for you.
How does tender matching work?
The app compares your Business DNA profile against all available tenders. It scores each tender based on sector match, budget fit, preferred methods, regions, and your risk appetite. High-scoring matches appear at the top of your Find Matches page.
What do the risk analysis metrics mean?
Risk Analysis (Premium feature) shows you: Buyer Integrity (how reliable the buyer is), Price Confidence (what range to bid), Timeline Risk (contract delays), Competition Level (how many bidders), and Bid/No-Bid recommendation. Each metric helps you decide whether to pursue a tender.
How do I upgrade my subscription?
Go to Learn & Upgrade → Upgrade in the navigation menu. Choose Free (basic matching), Pro ($20/mo with predictions and contacts), or Premium ($75/mo with full risk analysis). You can pay via card (Stripe), MTN MoMo, or bank transfer.
Is my data secure?
Yes. Your Business DNA and saved tenders are stored in a private Google Sheet accessible only to you. Payment processing is handled by Stripe and MTN MoMo — we never store your card or mobile money details. See our Privacy Policy for details.
What do the plan tiers include?
Free: 25 matches, AI tutor, knowledge base. Pro ($20/mo): Win predictions, competitor names, buyer contacts, price recommendations, calendar, 500 saved tenders. Premium ($75/mo): Full risk analysis, buyer integrity scores, contract analysis, sector reports, unlimited matches.
I found a bug or need help. Who do I contact?
Email the administrator at geraldnoria@gmail.com. For urgent issues, describe what page you were on and what went wrong. The admin can also approve bank transfer payments and manage subscriptions.

Quick Tips

  • Update your Business DNA whenever your company changes (new certifications, bigger budget). Go to My Tools → My Business DNA.
  • Save tenders you're interested in — they appear on your Dashboard for quick access.
  • Use the Risk Analysis tab on Check Tender to get a full picture before bidding.
  • Generate matches regularly to see new opportunities as tenders are added.
  • The Market Dashboard shows top sectors and buyers — great for strategic planning.
