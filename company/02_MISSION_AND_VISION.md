\# Our Purpose



EazyPickins Ltd exists to help organizations make better decisions.



Across Africa, governments, businesses, NGOs, universities, and development partners often have access to large amounts of information but struggle to transform that information into timely, reliable, and actionable decisions.



Our purpose is to bridge that gap.



We design platforms that convert fragmented data, documents, regulations, market signals, and operational knowledge into structured intelligence that decision-makers can trust.



We believe that better decisions create stronger institutions, more competitive businesses, greater transparency, and lasting social and economic impact.



\---



\# Our Mission



To build practical intelligence platforms that transform information into clear, reliable, and actionable decisions for African organizations.



We accomplish this by combining research, software engineering, automation, data systems, and human expertise into solutions that simplify complexity rather than increase it.



\---



\# Our Vision



To become Africa's most trusted builder of intelligent decision platforms.



We envision a future in which every organization—from a small business in Kigali to a national ministry, university, hospital, or multinational enterprise—can make faster, more confident, and evidence-based decisions using technology developed in Africa for African realities.



\---



\# Our 20-Year Ambition



Within the next two decades, EazyPickins Ltd aims to become a continental leader in decision intelligence by:



\- Building a portfolio of specialized intelligence platforms.

\- Serving organizations across multiple African countries.

\- Becoming a trusted strategic partner for public and private institutions.

\- Developing reusable intelligence frameworks that can be adapted across industries.

\- Demonstrating that African technology companies can lead globally in practical innovation.



Our goal is not simply to grow in size, but to increase the quality and impact of decisions made by the organizations we serve.



\---



\# What We Believe



We believe:



\- Information alone is not enough.

\- Data has little value unless it leads to action.

\- Simplicity is a competitive advantage.

\- Trust is earned through consistent reliability.

\- Knowledge should compound over time.

\- Technology should enhance human judgement, not replace it.

\# Our Strategic Direction



Every platform developed by EazyPickins contributes to one long-term objective:



> \*\*To create an ecosystem of decision intelligence platforms that work together while remaining valuable on their own.\*\*



Each product addresses a different decision domain, yet shares common principles, architecture, and standards.



Examples include:



\- Procurement Intelligence

\- Agricultural Intelligence

\- Health Intelligence

\- Education Intelligence

\- Financial Intelligence

\- Human Capital Intelligence

\- Environmental Intelligence

\- Regulatory Intelligence

\- Business Strategy Intelligence



This shared ecosystem allows knowledge, methods, and technologies developed in one domain to strengthen every other platform.

\# The Role of UmucyoAssistant



UmucyoAssistant is the flagship platform of EazyPickins Ltd.



It demonstrates the company's approach to decision intelligence by helping procurement professionals and organizations:



\- identify opportunities,

\- understand regulations,

\- evaluate risks,

\- monitor markets,

\- strengthen compliance,

\- build organizational knowledge,

\- and make better procurement decisions.



Beyond its direct value, UmucyoAssistant serves as the foundation upon which future intelligence platforms will be developed.
