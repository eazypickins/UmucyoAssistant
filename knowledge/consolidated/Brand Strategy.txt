## Prompt for your AI to implement the new feature

Use this as the implementation specification:

> **Feature: Tender Readiness & Compliance Dashboard**
>
> Add a new module called **Tender Readiness Dashboard** that transforms the platform into a complete procurement readiness system rather than only a tender search and proposal-writing tool.
>
> ### Objective
>
> Help organizations remain continuously ready to bid for opportunities by monitoring mandatory documents, compliance status, and organizational readiness.
>
> ### Functional Requirements
>
> 1. Create a secure company profile where users upload key registration and compliance documents (e.g., company registration, tax clearance, social security compliance, business licenses, professional certifications, and any organization-specific documents).
>
> 2. Extract document metadata using OCR or structured input where appropriate, including:
>
>    * Document type
>    * Issue date
>    * Expiry date
>    * Issuing authority
>    * Document number
>
> 3. Build a **Tender Readiness Score (0–100)** calculated from configurable factors such as:
>
>    * Required documents uploaded
>    * Document validity
>    * Missing mandatory items
>    * Expiring documents
>    * Company profile completeness
>    * Previous proposal quality metrics
>
> 4. Display a dashboard with:
>
>    * Overall readiness score
>    * Green/Amber/Red readiness indicator
>    * Missing documents
>    * Documents expiring within 90, 60, and 30 days
>    * Recently expired documents
>    * Recommended next actions
>
> 5. Create an automated reminder engine that sends email and WhatsApp notifications before document expiry (90, 60, 30, 14, 7, and 1 day).
>
> 6. Integrate the dashboard into the tender discovery workflow. Before a user starts preparing a proposal, automatically check readiness and highlight any missing or expired requirements that could affect submission.
>
> 7. Add a "Pre-Submission Compliance Check" that reviews both the proposal and the company's readiness profile, generating a checklist of potential issues before submission.
>
> 8. Store all uploaded documents securely with role-based access, encryption at rest, audit logs, and version history. Allow users to replace documents while retaining historical records.
>
> 9. Provide an administrative interface for configuring readiness scoring rules, required document types, notification schedules, and country-specific compliance requirements to support future expansion beyond Rwanda.
>
> ### User Experience
>
> The dashboard should feel like a mission control center. Users should immediately understand whether they are ready to bid, what needs attention, and what actions to take. The interface should prioritize clarity, visual indicators, and actionable recommendations over complexity.
>
> ### Non-Functional Requirements
>
> * Responsive design for desktop and mobile.
> * Secure authentication and authorization.
> * Scalable architecture for multi-tenant SaaS deployment.
> * Modular design so the readiness engine can be reused across multiple countries.
> * Comprehensive logging, monitoring, and automated testing for all core workflows.

## One recommendation I'd make before you launch

I would also rename the product.

A name centered on **"AI tender writer"** will eventually limit you because the platform already does much more.

Choose a name that can grow into the definitive procurement platform for East Africa. Your long-term vision could look something like this:

* **Discover** the right tenders.
* **Prepare** winning bids.
* **Validate** compliance before submission.
* **Learn** procurement best practices.
* **Monitor** organizational readiness.
* **Win** more contracts.
* **Analyze** performance over time.

That positions you not as another AI tool, but as the operating system for procurement success.

Finally, I strongly recommend we treat this as building a real go-to-market asset. Rather than a single document, we can create a **professional Procurement Success Playbook** (approximately 80–120 pages) with branded diagrams, execution checklists, outreach email templates, webinar scripts, LinkedIn posts, WhatsApp campaigns, KPI dashboards, and a 12-month implementation roadmap. It would be detailed enough for you—or even a future marketing team—to execute step by step without having to invent the strategy as you go.
