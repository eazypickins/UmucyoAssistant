# Chapter 10

# Sales, Customer Success & Revenue Operating System

## Introduction

The purpose of the sales function is not to persuade organizations to purchase software.

Its purpose is to help organizations improve their procurement performance.

This distinction is important.

Organizations purchasing UmucyoAssistant are making an investment in business growth. Every interaction should reinforce that investment by demonstrating measurable value.

The commercial operating model therefore combines consultative selling, structured onboarding, proactive customer success, and continuous value delivery.

---

# Commercial Philosophy

Every customer relationship should follow the same progression:

Discover

↓

Diagnose

↓

Demonstrate

↓

Deploy

↓

Develop

↓

Delight

↓

Refer

This becomes the standard operating framework for every member of the commercial team.

---

# Sales Principles

Every conversation should follow three rules.

### Rule 1

Lead with business outcomes.

Do not begin by explaining features.

Instead discuss:

* missed opportunities,
* proposal quality,
* procurement readiness,
* growth objectives.

---

### Rule 2

Teach before selling.

Customers should leave every meeting having learned something valuable, regardless of whether they subscribe.

This builds trust.

---

### Rule 3

Quantify value.

Whenever possible, estimate potential impact.

Examples include:

Hours saved.

Additional opportunities discovered.

Reduction in proposal preparation time.

Reduction in compliance risk.

Potential increase in bid participation.

---

# Customer Acquisition Pipeline

Every prospect should progress through a structured pipeline.

### Stage 1

Prospect Identified

The organization is added to the CRM.

Information captured includes:

Industry.

Company size.

Current procurement activity.

Decision-maker.

Source.

---

### Stage 2

Initial Contact

Objective:

Understand the organization's procurement process.

Avoid product demonstrations during the first conversation.

Focus on understanding challenges.

---

### Stage 3

Procurement Readiness Assessment

The prospect completes the assessment.

Outputs include:

Readiness score.

Procurement maturity level.

Recommended improvements.

Personalized demonstration agenda.

This transforms the sales conversation into a consulting engagement.

---

### Stage 4

Live Demonstration

Demonstrate only features relevant to the customer's priorities.

Examples:

Tender discovery.

Proposal review.

Historical procurement insights.

Compliance monitoring.

Procurement Pulse.

The demonstration should answer:

"How does this improve our procurement performance?"

---

### Stage 5

Trial Period

Recommended duration:

Fourteen days.

Objectives:

Receive personalized tender alerts.

Upload company documents.

Review an existing proposal.

Complete one Academy lesson.

Generate one Procurement Intelligence report.

The trial should guide customers toward meaningful outcomes rather than passive exploration.

---

### Stage 6

Subscription

Before payment, summarize the value already experienced during the trial.

Reinforce measurable improvements rather than listing features.

---

### Stage 7

Customer Success

Immediately after subscription, schedule:

Welcome meeting.

Readiness review.

Success planning session.

Thirty-day check-in.

Ninety-day business review.

---

# CRM Design

The CRM should track more than sales.

It should capture the customer's procurement journey.

Suggested fields include:

Industry.

Organization size.

Primary services.

Preferred procurement categories.

Current procurement maturity level.

Readiness score.

Workshop attendance.

Webinar attendance.

Proposal reviews completed.

Academy courses completed.

Procurement Pulse engagement.

Renewal probability.

Referral activity.

This creates a richer understanding of customer progress over time.

---

# The Customer Success Score

Develop an internal Customer Success Score combining indicators such as:

Platform activity.

Proposal reviews.

Readiness improvements.

Academy participation.

Document completeness.

Newsletter engagement.

Community participation.

Customers with declining scores should receive proactive support before they consider cancelling.

---

# Quarterly Business Reviews

Every three months, provide customers with a structured review.

Suggested sections:

Opportunities identified.

Proposals prepared.

Proposal quality improvements.

Readiness progress.

Academy participation.

Historical procurement insights relevant to their industry.

Recommendations for the next quarter.

This reinforces the perception that UmucyoAssistant is a strategic partner rather than a software subscription.

---

# Renewal Strategy

Never wait until the subscription expires.

Begin demonstrating value continuously.

Thirty days before renewal:

Share:

Procurement activity summary.

Business impact.

Readiness improvements.

Upcoming opportunities.

New platform features.

Customers renew when they clearly understand the value already received.

---

# Referral Programme

Satisfied customers are the most credible marketing channel.

Create a structured referral programme.

Rewards may include:

Additional users.

Extended subscriptions.

Academy credits.

Premium intelligence reports.

Recognition within the community.

The objective is to encourage advocacy without undermining trust.

---

# Customer Health Dashboard

Every customer account should display:

Readiness score.

Procurement maturity level.

Proposal quality trend.

Community participation.

Training progress.

Platform engagement.

Renewal status.

Recommended next actions.

This dashboard enables proactive account management.

---

# Commercial KPIs

Measure:

Lead-to-assessment conversion.

Assessment-to-demo conversion.

Demo-to-trial conversion.

Trial-to-paid conversion.

Customer Success Score.

Monthly recurring revenue.

Net revenue retention.

Customer lifetime value.

Referral rate.

Procurement maturity improvement.

These indicators provide a balanced view of commercial performance.

---

# Long-Term Objective

The ultimate measure of commercial success is not software adoption.

It is whether customers consistently become more capable procurement organizations.

When customers view UmucyoAssistant as an essential contributor to their business growth, recurring revenue becomes the natural consequence of sustained value delivery.
