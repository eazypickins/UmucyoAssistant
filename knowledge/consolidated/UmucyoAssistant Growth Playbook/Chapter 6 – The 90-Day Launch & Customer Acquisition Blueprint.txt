# Chapter 6

# The First 90 Days

## Introduction

The objective of the first ninety days is not rapid scale.

The objective is to establish trust, validate messaging, collect customer feedback, generate measurable success stories, and build a repeatable acquisition engine.

A small number of highly successful customers is significantly more valuable than a large number of disengaged trial users.

Every activity during this period should contribute toward one of four goals:

* Establish credibility.
* Generate qualified leads.
* Convert early adopters.
* Produce reference customers.

---

# Launch Objectives

By Day 90, UmucyoAssistant should aim to achieve:

* 100 registered organizations.
* 25–40 paying customers.
* At least 10 documented customer success stories.
* One active WhatsApp community.
* Three strategic partnerships.
* Three university engagements.
* Six educational workshops.
* Three public webinars.
* A weekly procurement newsletter.
* The first Procurement Intelligence Report.

---

# Success Philosophy

The first customers are not simply users.

They become:

* advisers,
* product testers,
* reference accounts,
* advocates,
* and future referral partners.

Their experience should receive exceptional attention.

---

# Execution Principle

Do not attempt to appear as the largest procurement company.

Instead, become the most helpful.

Organizations remember businesses that consistently solve problems before asking for a sale.

That reputation compounds over time and becomes a competitive advantage that advertising alone cannot purchase.
