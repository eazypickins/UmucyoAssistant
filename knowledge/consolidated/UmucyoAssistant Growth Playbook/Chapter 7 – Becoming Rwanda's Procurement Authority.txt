# Chapter 7

# Becoming Rwanda's Procurement Authority

## Introduction

Every industry has organizations that become the trusted source of knowledge.

People seek their opinions before making decisions.

Journalists quote them.

Universities invite them to speak.

Business associations ask them to train members.

Customers recommend them.

The goal of UmucyoAssistant is not merely to become the most widely used procurement software.

The goal is to become the organization businesses trust most when they need procurement knowledge.

Authority is one of the few competitive advantages that compounds over time.

---

# The Authority Strategy

Traditional marketing asks:

"How do we convince people to buy?"

Authority marketing asks:

"How do we become the organization people already trust before they decide to buy?"

The second approach is significantly more sustainable.
