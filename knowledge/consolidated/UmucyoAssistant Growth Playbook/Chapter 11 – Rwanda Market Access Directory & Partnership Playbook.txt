# Chapter 11

# Rwanda Market Access Directory & Partnership Playbook

## Introduction

The purpose of this chapter is to identify the organizations, institutions, business communities, and events that can accelerate UmucyoAssistant's adoption across Rwanda.

Rather than relying primarily on paid advertising, UmucyoAssistant should build a network of trusted partners that continuously introduce the platform to new businesses.

Each partnership should provide value first through education, research, and capability building.

---

# Partnership Prioritization Framework

Every potential partner should be scored using five criteria (1–5):

* Access to SMEs
* Procurement relevance
* Decision-maker accessibility
* Potential event frequency
* Long-term strategic value

Organizations with the highest combined scores should receive priority.

---

# Tier 1 – Strategic National Partners

## 1. Private Sector Federation (PSF)

**Priority:** Very High

**Strategic Value:** ★★★★★

### Why It Matters

PSF is Rwanda's principal business membership organization. It provides advocacy, networking, trade promotion, SME support, and organizes the Rwanda International Trade Fair (Expo). Its chamber structure gives access to thousands of businesses across multiple industries.

### Decision Makers

* CEO
* Director of Membership
* Chamber Executives
* SME Centre Leadership

### Offer

* Quarterly Procurement Masterclass
* Procurement Readiness Assessments for members
* Exclusive member discounts
* Annual Procurement Intelligence Report
* Joint webinars

### Success KPI

* 2 workshops per quarter
* 100 member assessments
* 20 paid subscribers each quarter

---

## 2. PSF SME Centre

**Priority:** Very High

The SME Centre already provides advisory services, diagnostics, capability development, networking opportunities, and business workshops for SMEs, making it an excellent distribution partner.

### Offer

"Become Procurement Ready"

A six-week programme covering:

* Tender readiness
* Proposal writing
* Compliance
* AI-assisted proposal review
* Market intelligence

---

## 3. Rwanda ICT Chamber

**Priority:** High

Technology companies are among the strongest early adopters because they are digitally mature and frequently pursue institutional contracts.

### Workshop

Winning More ICT Contracts Through Procurement Intelligence

Expected Result:

Develop your first cohort of customer success stories.

# Events Strategy

## Annual Priority Events

### Rwanda International Trade Fair (RITF)

Organized by PSF, this is Rwanda's largest annual trade exhibition and attracts hundreds of exhibitors and large visitor numbers from Rwanda and abroad. It provides excellent visibility for demonstrations, lead generation, and partnerships.

Objective:

* Live demonstrations
* Procurement health checks
* Lead capture
* Product launches

---

### SME & Entrepreneurship Conferences

Attend events focused on entrepreneurship, financing, innovation, and SME growth.

Examples include forums that bring together SMEs, investors, financial institutions, and policymakers around market access and business growth.

Objective:

Become the recognized procurement intelligence expert at every SME-focused event.

# Outreach Sequence

Week 1

Research the organization.

Week 2

Send a personalized introduction.

Week 3

Request a discovery meeting.

Week 4

Deliver a free educational session.

Week 5

Present a partnership proposal.

Week 6

Launch the first joint activity.

Always lead with value before discussing the software.

---

# Partnership Dashboard

Review monthly:

* Meetings booked
* Active partnerships
* Workshops delivered
* Businesses reached
* Webinar registrations
* Trials started
* Paying customers
* Revenue generated
* Customer retention from each partner

This allows UmucyoAssistant to identify the highest-performing distribution channels and invest more heavily in those relationships.

# Long-Term Objective

Within five years, UmucyoAssistant should be embedded in Rwanda's entrepreneurial ecosystem.

Business owners should encounter the company through associations, universities, incubators, workshops, newsletters, conferences, and trusted advisers long before they search for procurement software online.

When procurement capability becomes part of Rwanda's SME development conversation, UmucyoAssistant will have achieved sustainable market leadership.
